Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2, 2026

1. Introduction

BuildRight Solutions ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We may collect the following personal information:

  • Name and surname
  • Contact details (phone number, email address, physical address)
  • Property details and location
  • Project requirements and specifications
  • Payment information
  • Communication history with our company

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring website

3. How We Collect Information

We collect information through:

  • Quote request forms on our website
  • Email communications
  • Phone calls and WhatsApp messages
  • In-person consultations and site visits
  • Contracts and agreements
  • Website analytics tools

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To provide quotations and estimates
  • To deliver construction and maintenance services
  • To communicate about projects, schedules, and updates
  • To process payments and maintain financial records
  • To improve our services and customer experience
  • To send marketing communications (with your consent)
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • To maintain records for warranty and guarantee purposes

5. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information based on:

  • Consent: You have given explicit consent for specific purposes
  • Contract: Processing is necessary for fulfilling our contractual obligations
  • Legal Obligation: We are required to process your data by law
  • Legitimate Interests: Processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests

6. Information Sharing and Disclosure

6.1 Third-Party Service Providers

We may share your information with trusted third parties who assist us in:

  • Payment processing
  • Material suppliers and subcontractors
  • Professional advisors (accountants, lawyers)
  • Insurance providers

6.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

6.3 What We Don't Do

We DO NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your information for marketing purposes without consent
  • Transfer your data internationally without appropriate safeguards

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Secure storage of physical and digital records
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security assessments
  • Employee training on data protection
  • Encryption of sensitive data

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Fulfill the purposes for which it was collected
  • Comply with legal, accounting, or reporting requirements
  • Honor warranty and guarantee obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

Typically, we retain client records for a minimum of 5 years after project completion, or as required by South African law.

9. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal information
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal requirements)
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your information for certain purposes
  • Right to Restriction: Request restriction of processing
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with the Information Regulator

10. Marketing Communications

10.1. We may send you marketing communications about our services, special offers, and updates.

10.2. You can opt-out of marketing communications at any time by:

  • Clicking the "unsubscribe" link in emails
  • Contacting us directly
  • Replying "STOP" to SMS messages

10.3. Opting out of marketing does not affect service-related communications.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

12. Cookies and Website Technologies

12.1. Our website uses basic cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience.

12.2. Cookies help us:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Understand how you use our website
  • Improve website functionality

12.3. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

13. Social Media

We maintain presence on social media platforms (Facebook, Twitter/X). Information you share on these platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies. We recommend reviewing those policies.

14. Photography and Testimonials

14.1. We may photograph completed projects for marketing purposes.

14.2. We will request your consent before using identifiable images or testimonials.

14.3. You have the right to withdraw consent for use of your images at any time.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on our website with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
